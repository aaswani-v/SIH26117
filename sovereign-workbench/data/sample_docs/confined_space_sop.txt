Confined Space Entry SOP — Section 4.2

Before any confined space entry at Unit 4, the following lockout-tagout
sequence must be completed in order:

1. Isolate and lock out all energy sources feeding the vessel, including
   electrical, pneumatic, and process line connections.
2. Verify zero-energy state using a calibrated tester before proceeding.
3. Attach lockout tags bearing the entrant's name and the time of lockout.
4. Test the internal atmosphere for oxygen level, lower explosive limit
   (LEL), and hydrogen sulfide (H2S) concentration. Log all three readings
   before entry is authorized.
5. Assign a dedicated attendant stationed at the entry point for the
   entire duration of the entry, with continuous communication to the
   entrant.

No entry is authorized until steps 1-4 are logged and step 5 is confirmed
in place.
