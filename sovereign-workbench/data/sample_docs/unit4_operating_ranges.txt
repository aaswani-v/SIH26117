Unit 4 Vessel Safe Operating Range Reference

Vessel pressure: normal operating range 140-175 psi. Readings above 175 psi
must be flagged for immediate engineer review. Readings above 190 psi
require unit shutdown per emergency procedure EP-12.

Wall temperature: nominal range is 310-340 degrees F. A reading more than 5%
above nominal should be flagged as a corrosion risk indicator, especially
if paired with a rising trend across consecutive inspections.

Corrosion probe readings: an upward trend across two or more consecutive
inspection cycles should be flagged even if the absolute value remains
within tolerance, since trend direction is often a leading indicator of
wall thinning before the value itself exceeds tolerance.
